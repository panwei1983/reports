Recent studies commonly discretize pretrained multimodal embeddings into semantic identifiers (SIDs), allowing the model to learn task-specific semantic representations for recommendation. However, existing methods still provide limited gains due to two major limitations. First, codebook assignment fails to preserve semantic relevance and discards fine-grained continuous signals in the original embedding space. Extensive experiments on two public datasets demonstrate that PID consistently improves CTR prediction and yields larger gains for long-tail items.
